# Historical Specialty effects — retired design material

All 92 effects are archived here. None is granted by Training. Source: system/blueprints/skills.js before the Training refactor. Future feature assignment is undecided.

## Board (`board`, combat)

Crash through contested space, claim footing, and make the deck belong to your crew.

Context: Boarding actions, melees on unstable ground, and entering defended zones.

- Rank 1 — **Hard Entry**: Enter a contested zone without giving the defender a free reposition.
- Rank 2 — **Bring the Line**: When you force entry, one willing ally may follow without losing tempo.
- Rank 3 — **Deck Claim**: Once per scene, turn a successful entry into immediate positional advantage.
- Rank 4 — **Tidebreaker**: When you board through resistance, lesser foes must yield ground or expose themselves.

## Shoot (`shoot`, combat)

Put a shot exactly where panic, smoke, and motion say it should not land.

Context: Firearms, thrown weapons, shipboard guns, and ranged attacks under pressure.

- Rank 1 — **Steady Sight**: Ignore one minor ranged complication such as smoke, sway, or partial cover.
- Rank 2 — **Cut the Gap**: On a strong hit, choose either extra harm or a clean setup for an ally.
- Rank 3 — **Hold Aim**: Once per scene, keep aim through sudden movement or environmental disruption.
- Rank 4 — **Dead Reckoning**: After studying a target's movement, your next ranged hit can also break cover or momentum.

## Cut (`cut`, combat)

Fight in reach, tear openings, and punish the heartbeat when a guard fails.

Context: Blades, hooks, close-quarters weapons, and punishing enemies who overextend.

- Rank 1 — **Find Meat**: Exploit an exposed target without spending extra positioning.
- Rank 2 — **Open Seam**: On a successful close attack, create a visible opening or remove a minor guard.
- Rank 3 — **Answer Blood**: Once per scene, convert a defensive mistake into a decisive counterstrike.
- Rank 4 — **Red Tempo**: When you drop or disarm a foe, immediately threaten another enemy in reach.

## Brace (`brace`, combat)

Plant your stance, absorb impact, and protect ground when the world tries to move you.

Context: Forced movement, knockback, collisions, shield walls, and protecting allies in tight quarters.

- Rank 1 — **Set Stance**: You may use Guard instead of Reflex against forced movement.
- Rank 2 — **Absorb Impact**: When succeeding on Guard against impact, reduce collision damage by 1.
- Rank 3 — **Hold the Line**: Once per scene, automatically resist knockback after a successful Guard check.
- Rank 4 — **Living Bulwark**: Adjacent allies gain +1 Guard against the same force while you Brace.

## Feint (`feint`, combat)

Sell a false opening, pull an enemy off rhythm, and punish the moment they believe you.

Context: Duels, misdirection in close combat, baiting reactions, and setting up precision strikes.

- Rank 1 — **False Tell**: After a successful Reflex check in a duel, create a brief opening for your next attack.
- Rank 2 — **Hook the Eye**: When a foe reacts to your bait, an ally may ignore that foe's cover or guard for one action.
- Rank 3 — **Punish the Bite**: Once per scene, turn an enemy's failed read into disadvantage on their next attack.
- Rank 4 — **Killing Invitation**: When your Feint exposes a target, your next Precision hit may also reposition them.

## Suppress (`suppress`, combat)

Control lanes with threat, pressure, and timing until movement becomes a costly choice.

Context: Covering fire, chokepoints, ship decks, retreats, and protecting fragile objectives.

- Rank 1 — **Pin the Lane**: Successful ranged attacks can hinder enemy movement through your watched lane.
- Rank 2 — **Make Them Choose**: A suppressed target must either move slowly or expose themselves to allied follow-up.
- Rank 3 — **No Clean Crossing**: Once per scene, maintain suppression after relocating within the same zone.
- Rank 4 — **Storm of Warnings**: When you suppress a group, one lesser enemy freezes unless a leader spends an action rallying them.

## Bargain (`bargain`, social)

Turn leverage, favors, and timing into a deal both sides can survive.

Context: Markets, treaties, settlement favors, ship contracts, ransom talks, and tense negotiations.

- Rank 1 — **Know the Price**: Identify the concession that would actually move the other side before terms are locked.
- Rank 2 — **Counterweight**: Once per settlement, reroll a negotiation after introducing a new favor, risk, or debt.
- Rank 3 — **Terms Beneath Terms**: On a successful deal, name one hidden obligation or quiet benefit attached to it.
- Rank 4 — **Everybody Owes Somebody**: Once per session, turn a failed deal into a costly but usable counteroffer.

## Command (`command`, social)

Speak with the weight that makes hesitation feel more dangerous than obedience.

Context: Crew orders, battlefield direction, intimidation through authority, and crisis leadership.

- Rank 1 — **Clear Order**: Give direction under pressure without losing tempo to confusion.
- Rank 2 — **Snap Rally**: On success, one ally may immediately reposition or steady themselves.
- Rank 3 — **Voice Over Panic**: Once per scene, rally a shaken group into decisive action.
- Rank 4 — **Captain's Gravity**: When your command lands, allies in earshot ignore the first minor fear or hesitation cost.

## Deceive (`deceive`, social)

Build a lie sturdy enough for someone else to stand on it before it breaks.

Context: False identities, hidden motives, misdirection, and covering illicit action.

- Rank 1 — **Clean Lie**: Keep one small inconsistency from immediately exposing your story.
- Rank 2 — **Planted Assumption**: On success, plant a false assumption the target acts on briefly.
- Rank 3 — **Second Face**: Once per scene, reveal that a prior statement was deliberate misdirection.
- Rank 4 — **Lie with a Spine**: When your deception is challenged, redirect suspicion toward a plausible rival explanation.

## Read (`read`, social)

Catch what people hide in posture, silence, timing, and the things they refuse to name.

Context: Interviews, negotiations, ambush tells, court intrigue, and reading a room.

- Rank 1 — **Want Beneath Words**: Ask what a person most wants from the current exchange.
- Rank 2 — **Pressure Point**: On success, identify a pressure point without escalating the conversation.
- Rank 3 — **Before the Knife**: Once per scene, catch a hidden motive before it becomes a problem.
- Rank 4 — **Room of Ghosts**: After observing a scene, name who is afraid, who is lying, or who has the most to lose.

## Perform (`perform`, social)

Turn attention into a tool, a veil, or a weapon with rhythm and ceremony.

Context: Crowds, rituals, distractions, reputation plays, and commanding attention.

- Rank 1 — **Hold the Room**: Hold attention long enough for an ally to act quietly.
- Rank 2 — **Move the Weather**: On success, shift a crowd's mood one step without direct argument.
- Rank 3 — **Public Myth**: Once per session, make a public performance reshape local reputation.
- Rank 4 — **Legend in Motion**: When you perform for a crowd, choose one listener who must reveal awe, fear, or desire.

## Threaten (`threaten`, social)

Make consequences vivid enough that surrender starts sounding like wisdom.

Context: Stand-offs, interrogation, deterrence, and forcing hesitation without immediate violence.

- Rank 1 — **Sharp Consequence**: Make a threat credible without immediately starting a fight.
- Rank 2 — **Choose Your Hurt**: On success, make the target choose retreat, concession, or escalation.
- Rank 3 — **Freeze the Hand**: Once per scene, freeze a lesser foe who understands the consequences.
- Rank 4 — **Terms of Fear**: When your threat controls a scene, enemies must pay a cost before targeting someone else.

## Navigate (`navigate`, exploration)

Find the route the map forgot and the storm has not swallowed yet.

Context: Sea routes, storm paths, jungle lines, urban shortcuts, and long journeys.

- Rank 1 — **Two Roads**: Know the safest obvious route and the fastest risky route.
- Rank 2 — **Bad Weather Map**: Reduce one travel complication when you prepared the route.
- Rank 3 — **Hidden Approach**: Once per journey, reveal a hidden approach or emergency path.
- Rank 4 — **Arrive Before Trouble**: When a route turns hostile, your group acts before the first travel complication fully lands.

## Scout (`scout`, exploration)

Move ahead of certainty and return with the truth before danger has teeth.

Context: Recon, watches, advance movement, ambush avoidance, and hidden approaches.

- Rank 1 — **First Look**: Return with one useful detail before the group commits.
- Rank 2 — **Blind Spot**: On success, mark a safe approach or an enemy blind spot.
- Rank 3 — **Trip the Trap Lightly**: Once per scene, avoid triggering the first discovered hazard.
- Rank 4 — **Nobody Saw You Leave**: When you scout unseen, one ally may follow your path without making the first exposure check.

## Track (`track`, exploration)

Follow pressure, spoor, habit, and absence until the quarry becomes a story you can read.

Context: Pursuits, missing people, beast trails, battlefield clues, and forensic traces.

- Rank 1 — **Fresh Sign**: Follow a fresh trail without losing ordinary travel pace.
- Rank 2 — **Weight of Passage**: On success, learn one useful fact about the target's condition or numbers.
- Rank 3 — **Cold Trail**: Once per hunt, recover a cold trail from a meaningful clue.
- Rank 4 — **Inevitable Step**: When a quarry tries to vanish, name what they had to abandon or risk to do it.

## Endure (`endure`, exploration)

Keep moving when hunger, weather, pain, and fear all vote against you.

Context: Forced marches, exposure, starvation, storm watches, and prolonged strain.

- Rank 1 — **One More Mile**: Delay the first minor consequence from exposure or fatigue.
- Rank 2 — **Shared Grit**: Share your preparation to protect one nearby ally from the same strain.
- Rank 3 — **Still Standing**: Once per expedition, keep functioning through a condition that should stop you.
- Rank 4 — **Weathered Soul**: When the group suffers hardship, you may take the worst of it and keep one ally clear.

## Climb (`climb`, exploration)

Make rigging, cliffs, wreckage, and walls into roads no one else noticed.

Context: Rigging, cliffs, ruins, walls, collapsing structures, and vertical escapes.

- Rank 1 — **Handhold Faith**: Move through a climb without needing special gear when handholds exist.
- Rank 2 — **Rope for the Rest**: On success, help another character cross the same vertical obstacle.
- Rank 3 — **Catch the Fall**: Once per scene, recover from a slip before it becomes a fall.
- Rank 4 — **High Ground Belongs to You**: When you gain vertical advantage, your next action from that position gains stronger effect.

## Dive (`dive`, exploration)

Work where breath is a timer and pressure turns every mistake into a debt.

Context: Underwater salvage, flooded ruins, pressure locks, and breath-limited action.

- Rank 1 — **Below the Chop**: Act underwater without losing your first meaningful action to disorientation.
- Rank 2 — **Grip in the Dark**: On success, secure one object or ally while retreating from pressure.
- Rank 3 — **Borrowed Breath**: Once per dive, ignore a breath or pressure complication long enough to finish the job.
- Rank 4 — **Deep Claim**: When you bring something back from dangerous water, learn one secret it carried down.

## Jury-Rig (`jury-rig`, utility)

Make broken things work just long enough for the next impossible minute.

Context: Temporary repairs, emergency tools, field improvisation, unstable mechanisms, and ugly miracles.

- Rank 1 — **Ugly Fix**: Create a temporary fix that survives the current scene, even with poor materials.
- Rank 2 — **Hands in the Fire**: Perform field repairs under pressure without a full toolkit.
- Rank 3 — **One More Breath**: Once per scene, make a broken device work for one decisive action.
- Rank 4 — **Impossible Patch**: When a field repair should collapse immediately, choose one extra scene or one extra use.

## Stabilize (`stabilize`, utility)

Stop the bleeding, panic, overload, or collapse long enough for choice to return.

Context: Emergency medicine, failing systems, panic spirals, riot escalation, and containment moments.

- Rank 1 — **Stop the Spiral**: Stop a worsening condition from escalating for the moment.
- Rank 2 — **Buy the Minute**: On success, buy enough time for a safer follow-up action.
- Rank 3 — **No One Falls Yet**: Once per scene, prevent a collapse, bleed-out, or panic break from triggering.
- Rank 4 — **Calm at the Center**: When you stabilize a crisis, one nearby ally clears a minor fear or shock condition.

## Calibrate (`calibrate`, utility)

Tune temperamental tools, engines, scopes, and rituals until exactness becomes possible.

Context: Sensitive instruments, firing solutions, engines, sensors, and controlled procedures.

- Rank 1 — **True the Needle**: Prepare a tool or system so the next user avoids a minor complication.
- Rank 2 — **Clean Tolerance**: On success, improve reliability or precision for the next linked action.
- Rank 3 — **Exact Result**: Once per scene, make a calibrated device produce an exact result under pressure.
- Rank 4 — **Perfect Window**: When timing matters, create a brief window where a prepared ally may act without interference.

## Attune (`attune`, arcane)

Listen for the mood of relics, spirits, storms, and places that remember blood.

Context: Relics, spirits, corrupted sites, ritual spaces, omens, and unseen pressure before it breaks open.

- Rank 1 — **First Resonance**: Sense whether a relic, spirit, or place is receptive, hostile, afraid, or hungry.
- Rank 2 — **Name the Current**: On success, ask one focused question about the unseen pressure present.
- Rank 3 — **Echo Before Thunder**: Once per scene, notice occult danger before it fully manifests.
- Rank 4 — **Speak in the Same Weather**: When you safely attune to a power, an ally may act as if they understood its mood too.

## Sift (`sift`, arcane)

Separate omen from fear, residue from rumor, and truth from the noise power leaves behind.

Context: Aftermaths, visions, relic traces, curses, haunted evidence, and magical crime scenes.

- Rank 1 — **Lingering Echo**: Extract one clear impression from supernatural residue.
- Rank 2 — **Salt from Smoke**: On success, separate true omen from noise, fear, or staged signs.
- Rank 3 — **The Missing Moment**: Once per session, recover a vital clue from an echo others would miss.
- Rank 4 — **Pattern Under the Pattern**: When you Sift a scene, ask what future consequence is already taking shape.
